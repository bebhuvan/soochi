The Central Food Grains Storage Portal (CFSP), maintained by the Department of Food and Public Distribution, tracks the storage infrastructure that underpins India's food security system. It provides data on central pool storage capacity — FCI-owned godowns, hired storage, and state government facilities — along with stock positions of wheat and rice across zones and states. For researchers studying India's Public Distribution System, CFSP is the supply-side complement to the NFSA Dashboard (which tracks the distribution/consumption side): together they provide a complete picture of how food grains move from procurement to storage to distribution. The portal is part of the broader Integrated Management of Public Distribution System (IM-PDS) digital infrastructure.
