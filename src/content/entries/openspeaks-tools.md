OpenSpeaks develops three small browser-based applications for community
language documenters and archivists. Subtitler edits captions for local media;
Tome records consent, provenance, participants and other oral-knowledge
metadata; and Bento inspects, organises, measures and compresses audio and video
collections.

The tools are designed to remain usable with slow or absent connectivity and
to keep private or sensitive recordings on the user's device. They interoperate
with Wikimedia Commons and specialist archive metadata formats. All three are
open source and free, but remain in alpha or beta and should be tested carefully
before being made part of a critical archival workflow.
