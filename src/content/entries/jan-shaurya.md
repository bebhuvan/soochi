Jan Shaurya works with girls from Banchhada and Bedia communities in western
Madhya Pradesh to interrupt intergenerational caste-based sexual exploitation.
Its programmes combine identification and protection with Garima learning
centres, school support, exposure visits, life-skills education, legal awareness,
mentoring and Balika Panchayats intended to give girls a public voice and greater
control over their futures.

This is sensitive administrative and programme information, not a population
estimate of trafficking or exploitation. Counts on protection, villages and
participants are reported by the organisation and should be attributed as such;
its annual reports are the better source for dated claims. Descriptions should
also avoid identifying individual children or treating caste identity as destiny.
