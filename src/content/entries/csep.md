The Centre for Social and Economic Progress (CSEP) is an independent, non-partisan public policy research think tank located in New Delhi (established in 2020 as the successor to Brookings India).

CSEP produces research across macroeconomic stability, financial sector regulation, clean energy transition, critical minerals supply chains, foreign policy, and health economics. Its scholars provide evidence-based policy briefs to government ministries, regulatory bodies, and international forums.
