The Centre for Budget and Governance Accountability (CBGA) is an independent non-profit research and advocacy organization based in New Delhi. It specializes in tracking public budgets, fiscal policy decisions, and government spending priorities through the lens of equity and social justice.

CBGA produces accessible analyses of annual Union and state budgets, examining allocations for health, education, children, women (gender budgeting), agriculture, and marginalized communities. It also collaborates with grassroots organizations to build public literacy around fiscal accountability.
