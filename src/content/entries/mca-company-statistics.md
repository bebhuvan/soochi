The Ministry of Corporate Affairs publishes recurring summaries derived from
the MCA21 registry. They describe new registrations, active and closed companies,
authorised and paid-up capital, age, economic activity and geographic
distribution, providing a useful administrative view of India's incorporated
business sector.

Registration is not the same as an operating establishment, employment or
output, and filing status can lag real business activity. This public statistics
area should also be distinguished from paid company-document inspection and
restricted record-level MCA data. The MCA site sometimes blocks automated
clients, so downloads may work in a browser even when programmatic access fails.
