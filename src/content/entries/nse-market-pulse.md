NSE Market Pulse is a short, practical publication used by analysts and civic economists for market sentiment, sector rotations, and liquidity regime shifts in India. It is not a long-run statistical series by itself, but its periodic commentary is useful for interpreting why interest rates, equity flows and financing conditions are shifting in a given quarter. For policy advocacy teams, it helps translate abstract macro risks into market-facing signals. For data practitioners, it complements RBI and Ministry statistics where market behavior needs explanatory context.
