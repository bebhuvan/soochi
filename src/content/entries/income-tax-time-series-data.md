The best public window into India's formal income distribution and tax base. The Time Series Data PDF covers direct tax collections (corporate, personal), taxpayer counts by income bracket, return filing by type, PAN allocations, and state-wise direct tax contributions. Essential for understanding the scale and limits of India's tax base.
