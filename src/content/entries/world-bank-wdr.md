The World Development Report is one of the most cited annual development publications and remains a strong source for conceptual framing. For India-focused work, its theme-by-theme analysis helps anchor local findings to a global governance and development conversation, especially in education, institutions and climate resilience chapters. It is particularly useful for policy proposals where you need a global benchmark narrative rather than only a country-level time-series.
