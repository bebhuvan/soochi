The Global Assessment Report tracks disaster risk trends and prevention policy with a practical focus on resilience over reaction. For civic and research teams in India, it helps situate recurring flood, heat and cyclone events inside a global risk-management framework rather than treating each event as an isolated emergency. Use it with disaster registries, flood maps and social protection data when building district-level preparedness briefs.
