IDLI Stack (Indian Data Liberation Initiative) is a managed hosting platform purpose-built for Indian impact organisations. It lets nonprofits deploy powerful open-source tools — ERPNext for accounting and operations, chat platforms for team communication, fundraising and CRM tools, website builders — with a single click, without needing server administrators or DevOps expertise. The platform handles hosting, uptime, maintenance, updates, and support. Backed by Rainmatter Foundation, Samagata Foundation, FOSS United, and Tata Trusts, and trusted by organisations like Vidhi Centre for Legal Policy, Project Defy, and Kutch Mahila Vikas Sangathan. For civic organisations that want the power of FOSS without the infrastructure burden, IDLI is the closest thing to a turnkey solution.
