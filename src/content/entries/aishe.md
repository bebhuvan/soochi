AISHE is the national census-style survey of universities, colleges and
stand-alone higher-education institutions. It records enrolment by programme
and social category, teaching and non-teaching staff, examination results,
facilities and finances, and publishes indicators such as gross enrolment
ratio, gender parity and pupil–teacher ratio.

The portal provides reports, downloadable tables and an interactive dashboard.
Institutions report their own data, and coverage or classifications can change
between rounds, so apparent year-to-year jumps should be checked against the
report's institutional coverage and methodology. Pair it with UDISE+ for a
broader view from school through higher education.
