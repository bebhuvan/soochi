Bhoomi College is a Bengaluru learning institution run by the K N A Foundation
for Education, a public charitable trust. It offers fellowships, short
programmes, conferences and workshops that connect ecological living with
holistic education, systems thinking and the social and economic assumptions
behind environmental crises.

Its campus is also used as a working learning environment, incorporating organic
gardening, solar power, rainwater harvesting, water recycling and lower-impact
building methods. Programmes and residential participation can be paid, so
"free" should not be inferred from its nonprofit status; public articles and
programme information remain openly readable.
