India has the highest number of road crash deaths globally, and this is the authoritative dataset for transport safety research, infrastructure planning, and public health analysis. Annual reports include granular breakdowns by state, vehicle type, cause, age group, and road classification. The 2023 edition covers ~4.8 lakh road accidents.
