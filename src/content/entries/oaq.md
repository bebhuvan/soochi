OAQ brings readings from CPCB, Airnet and Aurassure monitors into one place, with a live map and charts for comparing air quality over time. Its focus is the neighbourhood scale: helping residents, researchers and community organisations see local PM2.5 and PM10 patterns that city-wide averages can hide. The platform documents a JSON API and licenses its data under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
