India's flagship direct income support scheme for farmers — ₹6,000 per year per eligible farmer family. The dashboard tracks fund transfers, beneficiary counts, eKYC completion rates, and state-wise implementation progress. An essential welfare-outlay tracker with unparalleled coverage of India's agricultural households.
