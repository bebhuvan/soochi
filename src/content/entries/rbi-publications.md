While the RBI's Database on Indian Economy (DBiE) is the statistical warehouse, the RBI Publications portal is where the central bank publishes its analytical and policy outputs. The most important regular publications include: the **Annual Report** (the RBI's definitive account of the economy each year), the **Financial Stability Report** (biannual systemic risk assessment), the **Report on Trend and Progress of Banking in India** (annual banking sector health check), the **Monthly Bulletin** (articles, speeches, and current statistics), the **Weekly Statistical Supplement** (high-frequency money and banking data), **State Finances** reports, and the **Working Papers** series. The portal also archives **speeches** by the Governor and Deputy Governors — often the first signal of policy shifts. All publications are freely downloadable as PDFs.
