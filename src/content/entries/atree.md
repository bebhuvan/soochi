ATREE (Ashoka Trust for Research in Ecology and the Environment) publishes the India Biodiversity Portal, runs Community Conservation Centres, the Ecoinformatics Lab, and the Lantana Craft Centre. Its Academy for Conservation Science offers PhD, MSc, and certificate programmes, and its knowledge commons approach translates ecological research into usable planning frameworks.
