The CDS is the single most important open climate data platform globally. ERA5 provides hourly climate and weather reanalysis from 1940 to near-present at ~31 km resolution, covering the atmosphere, land surface, and ocean. Also hosts C3S seasonal forecasts, CMIP6 climate projections, and sectoral applications (energy, water, agriculture, health). Free registration required for API access.
