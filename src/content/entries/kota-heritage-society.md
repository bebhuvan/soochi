Kota Heritage Society works for heritage preservation in Rajasthan and surrounding areas. Samagata Foundation backs its Marwar Block Print Project, which aims to document, revive, and publish approximately 400 culture-specific hand block prints from Western Rajasthan. The project recreates old designs using unique natural dye recipes, indigenous printing techniques, and artisan exchanges, with knowledge shared with academic institutions, museums, and printer communities.
