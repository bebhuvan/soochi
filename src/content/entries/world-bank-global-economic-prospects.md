Global Economic Prospects is the World Bank’s benchmark for macro scenario planning in emerging economies, with scenario-based forecasts and risk commentary for climate, trade and debt trajectories. For India, it is useful when comparing domestic growth assumptions against world markets, commodity risk and capital flow sensitivity. In advocacy, it often helps explain why climate-related shocks and financing conditions can quickly alter poverty and service-delivery outcomes if domestic buffers are weak.
