The Periodic Labour Force Survey is the main recurring official source for
employment, unemployment and labour-force participation in India. Its
person- and household-level files support analysis by sex, age, education,
industry, occupation, employment status, earnings and rural or urban location;
published reports provide the standard headline indicators.

The survey design was revamped from January 2025 to produce more frequent
all-India and state-level estimates. Results from the redesigned series should
therefore not be joined mechanically to older rounds: check the reference
period, status measure and MoSPI comparability notes before making a trend.
