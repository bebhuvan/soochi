Operational WeatherBench (OWB) is an open-access benchmarking platform developed by Brightband PBC that provides daily skill rankings for machine learning weather prediction models alongside operational NWP baselines.

Scoring models like WeatherNext 3, GraphCast, Aurora, and AIFS against ECMWF and NOAA GFS analysis fields, OWB provides objective meteorological verification (RMSE, ACC, CRPS) across key atmospheric levels.
