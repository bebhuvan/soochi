TRAI's performance-indicator series gives a recurring statistical view of fixed
and mobile telephony, internet and broadband, broadcasting and related services.
Reports include subscriber bases, wireless and wireline shares, rural–urban and
service-area teledensity, usage, revenue and market structure; separate
monitoring reports cover provider performance against quality-of-service norms.

Subscriber counts can include inactive or multiple SIMs and should not be read
as unique people. Definitions, reporting providers and service-area boundaries
change over time, while national aggregates can hide large local gaps. For trend
work, retain the report quarter and consult corrigenda rather than scraping a
single headline table.
