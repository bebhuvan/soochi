Navya Jeevan Foundation is a recently incorporated Delhi nonprofit that says it
has conducted community initiatives since 2021. Its current work is described as
food and essentials distribution, education support, skill-building and awareness
programmes intended to help women become more independent and to support children,
older people and other under-resourced communities.

The organisation was formally registered only in April 2026, so there is not yet
a long public record from which to assess continuity or impact. Its website also
says both that residential care is a future goal and that it currently provides a
home for orphaned children. Soochi does not repeat that contradictory claim; users
considering support or referral should confirm services directly.
