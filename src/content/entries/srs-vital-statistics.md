This Census of India catalogue brings together the Sample Registration System,
the Civil Registration System and medical certification of cause-of-death
publications. Use it for birth and death rates, fertility, infant and under-five
mortality, maternal mortality, life tables and registered causes of death, with
national and varying levels of state or rural–urban detail.

These systems measure different things. SRS estimates vital rates from a sample;
CRS counts registered events and depends on registration completeness; MCCD
covers medically certified deaths rather than every death. Keep the system and
its denominator visible when comparing states or building a time series.
