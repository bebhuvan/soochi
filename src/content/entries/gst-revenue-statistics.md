The government's own high-frequency pulse of formal economic activity. Monthly releases include total GST revenue, CGST, SGST, IGST, cess, state-wise breakup, and return filing counts. The GSTN portal (gst.gov.in) also publishes detailed registration and filing statistics. Arguably the single most-watched monthly economic release in India after CPI.
