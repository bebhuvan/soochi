FRLHT is a Bengaluru research organisation working to document and revitalise
India's medicinal heritage. Its work brings together traditional health
knowledge, medicinal-plant conservation, community practice and research across
Ayurveda, life sciences and public health, including digital documentation and
geospatial knowledge about threatened species.

It is recognised by government science bodies as a scientific research
organisation and has served as a national centre of excellence for medicinal
plants and traditional knowledge. Some of its databases and publications are
maintained through the associated ENVIS centre, so users should distinguish the
organisation from individual catalogues hosted under its domains.
