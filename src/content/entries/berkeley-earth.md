Berkeley Earth was founded to address concerns about bias in the main global temperature records. It incorporates more station data than any other group (over 50,000 stations), uses a transparent and fully reproducible methodology, and publishes uncertainty ranges with every estimate. Produces global and country-level temperature series, gridded fields, and city-level warming attribution. Widely cited in climate-litigation and climate-attribution work.
