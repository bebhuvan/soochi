The Local Government Directory is the Government of India's reference register
for administrative and local-government units. It assigns codes to states,
districts, subdistricts, villages, panchayats, urban local bodies and wards, and
publishes mappings among them and to constituencies. These identifiers are often
the best available keys for joining datasets from different departments.

Downloads can be generated for one state or for all entities, while services
also expose structured formats. The interface may require a captcha, and
boundaries and codes change as governments create or reorganise units. Preserve
the download date and use the modification reports when maintaining longitudinal
crosswalks.
