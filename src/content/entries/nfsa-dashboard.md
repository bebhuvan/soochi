The National Food Security Act portal links national and state reporting on
ration cards, beneficiaries, fair-price shops, depots, foodgrain allocation and
supply-chain performance. Its companion Annavitran system tracks portability and
distribution transactions under One Nation One Ration Card, while district
reports lead into state public-distribution systems.

This is federated administrative data: state systems, update dates and report
definitions differ, and beneficiary records are not a direct measure of food
consumption or nutritional outcomes. Counts can move because cards are added,
deleted, transferred, seeded or cleaned. Avoid publishing personally identifying
beneficiary details even when a state portal exposes them.
