This independent compilation harmonises nineteen editions of the Ministry of
Health and Family Welfare's Rural Health Statistics into 863 indicators. The
explorer covers facilities, health workers, infrastructure, coverage and tribal
areas, while preserving boundary changes and special values from the reports.
The underlying CSV and XLSX files are currently available only by request, so
the entry is classified by its openly accessible explorer rather than as a
bulk-downloadable dataset.
