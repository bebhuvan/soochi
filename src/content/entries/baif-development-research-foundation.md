BAIF Development Research Foundation was founded in 1967 by Manibhai Desai (a disciple of Mahatma Gandhi) at Uruli Kanchan, Maharashtra. BAIF is one of India's largest non-governmental rural development organizations, operating across more than 16 states.

BAIF is widely known for establishing the *wadi* model — transforming degraded tribal land plots into productive family orchards combining fruit trees, timber, and intercrops — as well as community-based livestock breeding, water resource development, and rural women's livelihood collectives.
