The SBM dashboard tracks urban sanitation infrastructure: individual and community toilets built, public urinals, solid waste processing plants, ODF/ODF+/ODF++/Water+ city certifications, and the annual Swachh Survekshan rankings. The rural counterpart (SBM-G) separately tracks village-level sanitation coverage and behaviour-change indicators.
