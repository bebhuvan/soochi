Global Trends is the baseline global briefing on displacement for advocacy, humanitarian planning and social protection design. It separates asylum, refugee and internally displaced populations and helps researchers track pressure on host countries and social systems, including neighbouring Indian contexts. The publication is particularly useful when linking migration policy work with city-level service planning and food-security or shelter assessments.
