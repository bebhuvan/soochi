Udhyam Learning Foundation works on entrepreneurship as a form of agency and
livelihood rather than only startup creation. Udhyam Shiksha partners with
government schools and vocational institutions to teach entrepreneurial
mindsets through practical projects; Udhyam Vyapaar works with street vendors,
home-based businesses and other nano-entrepreneurs on skills, tools, markets and
business resilience.

The organisation publishes annual reports, programme research, FCRA material
and audited financial statements, making those better sources for dated scale or
income claims than the changing homepage counters. Participation and outcomes
are programme-specific: a student reached through a state curriculum and a
vendor receiving intensive support should not be counted as equivalent forms of
engagement.
