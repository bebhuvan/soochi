TradeStat is the Department of Commerce and DGCI&S dissemination system for
India's merchandise trade. It supports queries across harmonised-system
commodities, partner countries, regions, ports and time periods, with quantity
and value measures useful for tracking exports, imports and trade balances.

Commodity classifications and revisions change over time, and country totals
can differ from partner-reported data because of valuation, timing and
classification. Record the HS level, currency, financial or calendar period and
whether values are provisional. The portal is for goods trade; services trade
requires other official sources such as RBI.
