Opened in 1997, RSCP hosts lakhs of visitors annually who interact with science and technology exhibits and watch space shows in its large 3D domed projection theatre. It runs exhibitions, activities, and programmes especially for children. Samagata Foundation co-funded a major revamp and modernisation of RSCP's infrastructure through an MoU with the government.
