Jagriti runs the annual 8,000 km Jagriti Yatra rail journey connecting young entrepreneurs with grassroots enterprises across India. Its Jagriti Enterprise Centre in eastern UP provides physical incubation and enterprise support infrastructure, using entrepreneurship as the vehicle for equitable development in underserved regions.
