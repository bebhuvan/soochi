The PMAY-U dashboard (PMAY Urban MIS) provides city, district, and state-level progress on India's flagship housing scheme — central assistance released, houses sanctioned, grounded, completed, and beneficiary details by social category. The rural vertical PMAY-G is tracked separately through the AwaasSoft platform. Together they cover India's largest housing welfare programme.
