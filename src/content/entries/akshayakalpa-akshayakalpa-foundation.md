Akshayakalpa turns organic dairy into a farmer-entrepreneurship system spanning farm training, closed-loop production, processing infrastructure, and direct-to-consumer products. Its co-existence farming system centres on self-sustaining farms, methane generation from dung, and closed-loop soil-fodder systems.
