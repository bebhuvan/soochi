The Labour Bureau collects retail wage rates across 46 occupations (12 agricultural, 7 plantation, 19 non-agricultural rural, and 8 urban) from 600 sample villages and urban centres. This is the most granular, high-frequency wage data available for rural India — essential for tracking agricultural distress, MGNREGA wage comparisons, and real purchasing power at the bottom of the income distribution.
