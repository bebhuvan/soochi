The Global Report on Food Crises is the FAO’s authoritative annual signal on food availability stress, price shocks, climate events, and policy responses at country level. It is one of the clearest global frames for advocacy work around drought, conflict-related supply shocks, and social protection response design. For India, it is most useful alongside state-level crop and poverty indicators to evaluate whether food inflation and household resilience risks are converging in the same time periods.
