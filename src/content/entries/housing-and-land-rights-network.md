Housing and Land Rights Network (HLRN), based in New Delhi, works for the recognition and realization of the human rights to adequate housing, land, and livelihood for marginalized rural and urban communities in India.

HLRN publishes the annual *Forced Evictions in India* national report, maintaining the primary public record of demolition drives, state displacement, and rehabilitation failures. The organisation provides legal aid, conducts human rights impact assessments, and advocates for rights-based urban housing and slum regularisation policies.
