Progress of the World’s Women is a strong periodic publication for advocacy and programme design because it combines gender outcomes with institutional and legal context. For India-focused work, it is useful for setting measurable equality targets in employment, safety, care burden and leadership outcomes when local administrative data is fragmented. It is best used alongside India-specific laws and state budgets to convert global benchmarks into actionable recommendations.
