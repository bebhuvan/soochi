The Open Contracting Partnership (OCP) is an independent non-profit organisation that works globally to make government public procurement open, transparent, and accountable. It stewards the Open Contracting Data Standard (OCDS), an internationally recognized open data schema for publishing public contracting data from planning to execution.

OCP works with governments, civic watchdog organizations, and journalists across more than 50 countries to open up procurement datasets, identify anti-competitive bidding patterns, and ensure public funds are spent equitably on essential civic infrastructure and services.
