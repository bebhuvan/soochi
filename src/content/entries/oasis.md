OASIS (Open-Source Alliance for Social Innovation & Sustainability) is a network of organisations united by the vision that Free and Open-Source Software can empower citizen sector organisations to create positive impact at scale. It addresses the large gap between the social sector and the availability of high-quality FOSS technology. Samagata Foundation is a founding member and patron organisation.
