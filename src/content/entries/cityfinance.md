Cityfinance is a city-level financial platform for comparing and analysing municipal performance across India. It brings together audited statements, municipal revenues, debt, and service level benchmarks in one place, with city-wise drilldowns for practical policy work. This is especially useful for teams focused on urban finance, fiscal devolution and state-level supervision of municipal accounts.

The strongest use case is not raw extraction of every table but rapid assessment and benchmarking: you can quickly identify financially stressed cities, compare income capacity signals, and validate which data are available in published municipal forms before moving to deeper analysis in government filings. It is most useful when paired with Open Budgets India and state finance portals during campaign briefs or city-focused policy work.
