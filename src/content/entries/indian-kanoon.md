Indian Kanoon provides free full-text search across Indian statutes, court
judgments and orders from the Supreme Court, High Courts, selected district
courts and tribunals. Documents are broken into clauses and connected through
citations, cited-by links, statutes, judges and courts, making it especially
useful for tracing how a provision has been interpreted. The core database and
search are free; alerts, research and AI tools have premium tiers, while API
use is metered with allowances for development and verified non-commercial
work.
