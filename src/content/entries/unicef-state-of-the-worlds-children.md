State of the World's Children gives child-focused evidence in a highly usable advocacy format: coverage of child mortality, learning outcomes, protection gaps and child wellbeing trajectories. For India, it helps support child-rights programmes by providing global comparators and a common language for outcome framing. It is most useful when paired with India-specific health systems and education administrative datasets, because it clearly separates symptoms (trends) from policy levers (programmes).
