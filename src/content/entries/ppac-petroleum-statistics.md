The Petroleum Planning and Analysis Cell publishes monthly and annual statistics
on crude oil, petroleum products and natural gas. Its tables and reports cover
domestic production, imports and exports, refinery throughput and capacity,
product consumption, LPG and fuel-market indicators, prices, taxes and energy
infrastructure; the Oil & Gas Ready Reckoner provides a compact sector overview.

Different tables use calendar years, financial years or monthly cumulative
periods, and preliminary figures may later be revised. Product categories and
units also vary—tonnes, kilolitres, standard cubic metres and energy equivalents
are not interchangeable. Preserve the unit, period and revision status when
combining series.
