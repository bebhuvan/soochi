NCF's public artefacts include Hornbill Watch (citizen-science initiative), Biodiversity Basics (online natural history course), and EcoQuest (nature discovery centre). Its CEROS platform brings together community ecology and restoration work across multiple biodiversity hotspots, and its Nature Guide Training Course creates livelihood pathways through conservation.
