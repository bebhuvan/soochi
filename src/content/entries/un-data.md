UNdata is the UN system's unified statistical portal, providing a single search interface across 35 UN databases — from population and national accounts to trade and energy. For India researchers, it aggregates country-level data from WHO, UNESCO, ILO, FAO, UNIDO, World Bank, IMF, and the UN Statistics Division. The key value is convenience: instead of visiting a dozen agency-specific portals, you can get demography, economy, health, education, and environment data for India from a single query. Data is downloadable in multiple formats. Particularly useful for quick cross-country comparisons and for finding data from smaller UN agencies that are harder to discover individually.
