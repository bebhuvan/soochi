Annadana is a registered nonprofit focused on conserving heirloom vegetable
seeds, regenerating soils and sharing low-cost natural-farming practices. Its
food-forest farms serve as learning and experimentation sites, while its seed
banks, training and distribution work are intended to keep traditional,
reproducible varieties in the hands of farmers and rural youth.

This is the conservation and education network, not a conventional commercial
seed catalogue. Its official website still contains substantial organisational
material, but the site's security certificate was expired when this entry was
checked. Soochi therefore marks it dormant until safe browser access is restored.
