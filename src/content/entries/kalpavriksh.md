Kalpavriksh is an Indian environmental action group whose work connects
biodiversity conservation with livelihoods, democratic governance and social
equity. Its programmes span community-conserved areas, environment and
development, urban ecology, environmental education and documentation of
alternatives to ecologically destructive models of development.

The organisation also publishes books, newsletters, research and teaching
material and convenes Vikalp Sangam. Soochi lists its Community Conserved Areas
Portal separately because that is a data resource in its own right; this entry
is for the wider organisation and body of civic and advocacy work.
