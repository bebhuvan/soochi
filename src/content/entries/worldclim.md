WorldClim provides high-resolution (1 km²) monthly climate surfaces — min/mean/max temperature, precipitation, solar radiation, wind speed, water vapour pressure — plus 19 derived bioclimatic variables that capture seasonality, extremes, and growing conditions. Also publishes downscaled CMIP6 future climate projections. The standard input for species distribution models, crop suitability studies, and conservation planning. Version 2.1 covers 1970-2000 normals.
