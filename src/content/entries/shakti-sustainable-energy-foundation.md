Shakti Sustainable Energy Foundation structures work across cooling, mobility, air quality, freight, climate finance, and city action. Its catalytic philanthropy model builds coalitions, evidence, and partner capacity so major public-policy shifts become possible. It helped build the analytical base for the India Cooling Action Plan.
