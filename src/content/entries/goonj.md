GOONJ's signature Cloth for Work model rewards locally led development work with dignified material support instead of treating money as the only development input. Key initiatives include MY Pad (cloth-based sanitary pad system), School to School (education material transfer), Rahat (disaster response), and Not Just a Piece of Cloth (menstrual health).
