In Development Magazine is an independent digital publication publishing narrative longform essays on economic progress, policy experiments, and institutional capability in low- and middle-income countries.

Supported by Emergent Ventures and the Effective Altruism Infrastructure Fund, the magazine pairs econometric rigor with on-the-ground reporting, exploring how governance, industrial strategy, and civic technology drive tangible human progress.
