WASSAN anchors the Odisha Millet Mission as programme secretariat, serves as national hub for the Revitalising Rainfed Agriculture Network, and anchors the National Coalition on Natural Farming. Its Practice-Research-Policy approach links rainfed landscapes to public systems.
