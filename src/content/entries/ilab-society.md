iLAB Society focuses on uplifting marginalised coastal communities in Kozhikode through education and leadership development. Its Coastal Education Enhancement Mission (CEEM) addresses high dropout rates and literacy gaps in fishing communities by providing quality primary education and life skills in students' mother tongue. Samagata Foundation supports CEEM to expand its reach. Programmes include MissionTEN, MILES, and Lifeskill Camps.
