FOSS United Foundation promotes and strengthens the Free and Open Source Software ecosystem in India. It organises events, conferences, and nurtures open-source software projects coming out of India. Samagata Foundation is a patron organisation supporting FOSS United with a long-term commitment. FOSS United is also a member of the OASIS alliance.
