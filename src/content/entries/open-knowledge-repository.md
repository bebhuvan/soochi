The Open Knowledge Repository is the World Bank’s main channel for open access publications and working papers. It is useful as a discovery layer when you need official working papers, policy briefs, conference papers and reports that may not yet be indexed in flagship publication pages. For India-centric research, it is especially useful for pulling background material, impact evaluations and sector-specific technical notes to support evidence-based writing.
