The Emissions Gap Report gives a global comparison of where mitigation action is falling short of temperature goals. For India, this is useful when discussing energy transitions, industrial policy and adaptation-versus-mitigation tradeoffs in development plans. The report is often used in policy discussions to support stronger local climate planning and to justify low-carbon interventions with comparable global evidence.
