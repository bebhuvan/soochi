GISTEMP is one of the four primary global temperature records (alongside HadCRUT, NOAA, and Berkeley Earth) that the IPCC and every climate assessment rely on. NASA GISS also publishes sectoral climate datasets: aerosols, clouds, radiation balance, and climate model outputs (NCCS). The GISTEMP station data maps are particularly useful for checking regional coverage quality.
