Bribes.fyi is a crowdsourced registry of encounters with bribery in public
services, including reports where a bribe was refused. The full set of approved
reports is available as CSV and JSON. Because contributors opt in, the data is
a self-selected record of incidents rather than a representative measure of
corruption prevalence.
