Svatah is a non-profit organisation based in Hyderabad dedicated to building financial literacy, informed decision-making, and long-term economic stability among marginalized communities, women self-help groups, youth, and school students.

Through initiatives like Svatah Bhavishya (curriculum-integrated financial education for middle-school students), Svatah Sakhi (household budget and savings training for women micro-entrepreneurs), and the Svadhyay digital learning portal, the organisation delivers localized workshops on banking access, formal credit, digital fraud prevention, and government social security schemes.
