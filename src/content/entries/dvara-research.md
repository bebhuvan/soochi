Dvara Research studies how low-income households and small enterprises can
access and use suitable financial services and social security. Its work spans
household finance, financial-system design, digital finance, customer
protection and social protection, combining policy research with field-based
testing of practical interventions.

The searchable repository includes research papers, policy and data briefs,
consultation responses, primers, reports and tools. The material is freely
available to read, but the site labels it all rights reserved rather than under
an open licence.
