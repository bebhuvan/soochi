Reap Benefit has a remarkably productised portfolio: Solve Ninja Bot (WhatsApp problem-solving tool), SamaajData (crowdsourced civic data), Samaaja (open-source engagement software), and the DISS framework (Discover-Investigate-Solve-Share). It is building a nationwide movement of first-mile changemakers through hyper-local civic and climate action.
