The Annual Survey of Industries is India's principal source on the registered
factory sector. Its public-use files and tables cover factories, workers and
wages, fixed capital, working capital, materials, fuels, electricity, products,
output and value added, with industry and state classifications suitable for
studying manufacturing structure and productivity.

ASI is not a census of every Indian enterprise: it covers factories within its
statutory frame, while most informal and unregistered establishments are covered
by separate enterprise surveys. Sampling schemes, NIC industry classifications
and disclosure treatment vary across years. Use multiplier weights and current
concordances rather than treating observed records as totals.
