HCES records household consumption of food, fuel, clothing, housing, education,
health, transport and other goods and services in both quantity and value terms.
It is the main official source for monthly per-capita consumption expenditure
and supports work on living standards, poverty, inequality, consumption baskets
and the weights used in consumer-price indices.

The 2022–23 and 2023–24 rounds followed a long gap and introduced design and
questionnaire changes, so they are not automatically comparable with older NSS
consumer-expenditure rounds. Unit-level files are split into multiple record
levels and require careful merging with the documentation, weights and survey
design; a simple row average will not reproduce the published estimates.
