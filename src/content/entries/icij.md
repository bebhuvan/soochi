The International Consortium of Investigative Journalists (ICIJ) is a Washington-based non-profit newsroom and global network of investigative reporters founded in 1997.

ICIJ is famous for pioneering massive, multi-newsroom data collaborative investigations into offshore financial secrecy and tax havens, including the Panama Papers, Paradise Papers, FinCEN Files, and Pandora Papers. ICIJ maintains the public Offshore Leaks Database, allowing citizens and researchers to search ownership networks of over 800,000 offshore companies and trusts.
