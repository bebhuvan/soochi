Sahjeevan's work spans milk and dairy value-chain interventions across cow, buffalo, camel, sheep, and goat products; community institutions and producer organisations; and Biological Management Committees with People's Biodiversity Registers. Its Communities-Institutions-Advocacy framework centres on commons governance and pastoral livelihoods.
