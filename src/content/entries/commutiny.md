ComMutiny has built a youth-development ecosystem around the idea of the Jagrik (jagruk nagrik — aware citizen). Its public products include the Be a Jagrik board game, the Togetherness Table dialogue format, and the vartaLeap cross-sector coalition for youth-centric development.
