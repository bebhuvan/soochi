Bharat Digital aims to define an archetype of a 'public technologist' — someone who instills citizen-centricity, design, and systems thinking when building technology inside government. Its flagship Build for Bharat Fellowship is a six-month programme training early-career technologists in applying technical expertise, design, and UX toward the public good within government systems. Backed by Samagata Foundation.
