Dalgo brings data from spreadsheets, survey tools, CRMs and programme systems
into an organisation-controlled warehouse. It automates ingestion and
transformation, then feeds cleaned data into dashboards, reports, alerts and
other applications so monitoring and evaluation teams spend less time
repeatedly consolidating files.

Project Tech4Dev developed the platform around the needs of social-impact
organisations, including integrations with KoboToolbox, Avni and Glific. Its
core backend and portal are available under the AGPL. Using the code is free,
but Dalgo's managed service and implementation support are paid and cloud
infrastructure carries its own costs.
