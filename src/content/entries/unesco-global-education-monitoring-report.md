The GEM report is a benchmark for education monitoring because it combines systems-level analysis, financing patterns and learning outcomes over time. In India-specific work, it is valuable for framing long-term education outcomes and identifying evidence areas where state-level performance diverges from national averages. It is also useful for organisations focused on skills, equity, and school retention because it links learning quality to broader social inclusion outcomes.
