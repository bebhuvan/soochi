The DDC is the reference point for every dataset cited in IPCC Assessment Reports. It archives observed climate records, CMIP5/CMIP6 global climate model output, downscaled regional projections, and the SSP (Shared Socioeconomic Pathway) scenario database. For India-specific analysis, the DDC's regional data pages provide pre-extracted South Asia time series and maps from each generation of climate models.
