Conducted by the International Institute for Population Sciences for the
health ministry. District-level factsheets are the reason so much Indian
health analysis is possible at all — almost nothing else official goes
below the state.

NFHS-6 (2023–24) fact sheets for India, the states and the union
territories are out. The unit-level data and the machine-readable series
arrive later and elsewhere — the survey is run by the health ministry but
served through MoSPI.
