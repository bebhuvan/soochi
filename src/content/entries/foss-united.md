FOSS United Foundation promotes and strengthens the Free and Open Source
Software ecosystem in India. It organises IndiaFOSS, FOSS Hack, local chapters
and meetups, supports maintainers, and conducts research and advocacy around
technology policy and open digital infrastructure.

Its grants programme funds Indian FOSS projects, maintainers, events, open data
and other digital commons work. The grants catalogue records supported
projects, while Forklore profiles Indian maintainers and the software they
build. FOSS United is also part of OASIS, a network working to increase the use
of open technology in social-impact organisations.
