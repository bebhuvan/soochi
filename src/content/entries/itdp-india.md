The Institute for Transportation and Development Policy (ITDP) India is a non-profit organization providing technical expertise and policy advocacy to promote equitable, sustainable, and climate-resilient urban transportation systems.

ITDP India works directly with central ministries and municipal city corporations to design Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) corridors, pedestrian-first "complete streets," dedicated non-motorized cycle track networks, and parking management policies. It also supports national initiatives like the India Cycles4Change and Streets for People Challenges.
