The Survey of India portal distributes authoritative national map products,
including administrative and village boundaries, topographic sheets, roads,
railways, settlements, hydrology and terrain. Depending on the product, files
are supplied as GIS databases or shapefiles, rasters and PDFs suitable for
mapping, planning and geographic reference.

Access is mixed: some boundaries and map sheets are free, while detailed
products may require registration, identity verification or payment. Download
conditions and permissible reuse differ by product, so do not treat the whole
catalogue as openly licensed. Also keep edition dates—an official boundary file
can still lag a recent administrative reorganisation.
