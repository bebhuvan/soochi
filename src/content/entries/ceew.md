The Council on Energy, Environment and Water (CEEW) is one of Asia's leading policy research institutes. Its public dashboards and tools — including the Electric Mobility Dashboard, India Renewables Dashboard, ValueSTAC solar calculator, and Industrial Energy Emissions Dashboard — convert sectoral research into operational public infrastructure.
