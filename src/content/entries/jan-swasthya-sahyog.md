Jan Swasthya Sahyog (JSS) is a voluntary organisation founded in 1999 by a team of health professionals to provide affordable, high-quality healthcare in rural and tribal Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh. JSS runs a rural referral hospital at Ganiyari, three outreach sub-centres, and a community health programme covering over 70 forest-fringe villages.

Beyond direct clinical care, JSS trains female village health workers, manufactures essential low-cost generic pharmaceuticals, operates a low-cost medical diagnostics workshop, and conducts epidemiological research on undernutrition, tuberculosis, and rural health disparities in tribal India.
