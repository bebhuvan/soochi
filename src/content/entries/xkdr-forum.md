XKDR Forum (Cross-Disciplinary Knowledge Data Research) is a Mumbai-based research group and think tank that investigates structural governance, economic, and institutional questions in India.

The forum combines economics, law, public administration, computer science, and statistics to build empirical evidence and open research infrastructure. Its key research verticals include state capability, judicial mechanics and court data analysis, Union and State debt sustainability, household finance, power sector transition, and computational public policy tools.
