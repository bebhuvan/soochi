Employment and Social Trends is the ILO’s flagship read on labour outcomes, including formal/informal employment splits, social protection gaps, youth transitions and productivity risks. For researchers in India, this report is useful for framing workforce trends that are not visible through a single ministry dashboard. It pairs well with PLFS and enterprise survey work when looking at employment quality, informality and decent work commitments. Because it covers many economies in one model, it is especially useful for comparative advocacy.
