MyNeta is a civic-facing index for election transparency in India, pulling candidate filings from the official system and presenting them in usable, comparable formats. It is especially useful for tracking declared criminal cases, assets, liabilities and education claims across candidates and elections, then linking that back to constituency-level accountability work.
