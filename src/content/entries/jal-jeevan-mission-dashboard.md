The Jal Jeevan Mission reporting system tracks functional household tap
connections in rural homes, schools and anganwadis. It offers state-to-habitation
reports on connections, piped-water villages, water quality, schemes, village
action plans, expenditure and the progression from reported to Gram
Sabha-certified Har Ghar Jal status.

The dashboard is administrative monitoring data updated by states and union
territories, not an independent household survey. A recorded connection does not
by itself establish regular flow, quantity or water quality, and "reported" is
distinct from "certified." Use the detailed dated reports and retain the portal's
reconciliation notices when constructing local time series.
