The official registry of startups recognised by DPIIT under the Startup India Action Plan. The public dashboard provides registration statistics by state, sector, and year, with filters for women-led startups and patent/trademark holders. Recognition enables startups to access tax exemptions, IPR fast-tracking, and public procurement benefits.
