ALT EFF (All Living Things Environmental Film Festival) is India's dedicated environmental film festival. It screens climate and nature films across venues in India, using cinema to make environmental issues emotionally resonant and publicly accessible. The festival has grown into a year-round programme of screenings, workshops, and community events.
