PMGSY is India's programme to provide all-weather road connectivity to unconnected rural habitations. The OMMS (Online Management, Monitoring and Accounting System) portal provides habitation-level connectivity status, road-wise construction progress, expenditure tracking, and GIS maps of the rural road network. For researchers, this is the definitive source for studying rural connectivity, infrastructure access, and the relationship between road construction and economic outcomes. The public dashboard provides state, district, and block-level summaries. Combine with Census habitation data for connectivity gap analysis.
