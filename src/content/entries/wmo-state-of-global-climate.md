The State of Global Climate publication series is widely used for climate baselines and extreme weather context in policy and budget discussions. For India teams, it is important for climate attribution in heat stress, rainfall variability and agriculture planning debates. The publication is not India-only but provides global framing and methodology that helps interpret local weather and climate anomaly datasets used in adaptation planning.
