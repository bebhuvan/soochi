IIHS produces the Urban Atlas for India (comparative land-use dataset for 100 cities), Knowledge Gateway (open research repository), Media Lab (audio-visual archive), and Geospatial Lab. Its Urban Fellows Programme, Word Lab, and Kengeri campus as living lab make it a comprehensive urban knowledge institution.
