Mongabay India is the India edition of the independent nonprofit environmental
news organisation. Its journalists report on forests, wildlife, oceans,
pollution, climate, energy, infrastructure and conservation, often connecting
scientific evidence and policy decisions with the experiences of affected
communities.

The publication is available in English and Hindi without a paywall. Its stated
Creative Commons Attribution–NoDerivatives licence permits republication with
attribution but not altered versions, so it should not be treated as unrestricted
open text. Soochi lists the publication rather than each individual report.
