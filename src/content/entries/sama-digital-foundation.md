Sama Digital Foundation is a Pune-based nonprofit working to close the digital divide by refurbishing donated laptops and e-waste devices and channelling them into digital and coding education for underserved women and youth, with a stated goal of reaching one million people by 2030.

It partners with organisations such as Navgurukul and Code to Enhance to run device donation drives and digital training programmes leading toward employment.
