MSSRF's infrastructure includes Village Resource Centres and Village Knowledge Centres, Fish for All Research and Training Centre, the Community Agrobiodiversity Centre and MSSBG in Wayanad, and the Biju Patnaik Tribal Agrobiodiversity Centre in Jeypore. Its approach positions science as a grassroots capability for resilient ecosystems, food security, and climate adaptation.
