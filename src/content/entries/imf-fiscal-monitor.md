The Fiscal Monitor tracks fiscal policy trajectories, debt servicing pressures, tax design, and fiscal policy trade-offs across advanced and emerging economies. The India-relevant sections are particularly useful for researchers writing on state-level fiscal stress, debt affordability, and medium-term budget design when local budget notes are delayed. It gives comparability across countries and a short-cycle read on macro-fiscal policy, making it a practical complement to India Budget documents and RBI financial aggregates for policy communication.
