Software Freedom Law Center, India (SFLC.in) is a donor-supported legal services organisation that works to protect civil liberties, digital rights, and free software in the digital domain. SFLC.in provides pro bono legal support to developers, civil society organizations, and citizens on issues concerning digital rights, privacy, censorship, and software licensing.

The organisation maintains internetshutdowns.in, the canonical database documenting telecom and internet network suspensions ordered across Indian states. It conducts empirical research, submits policy briefs on data protection and intermediary liability, and intervenes in key constitutional challenges around online freedom of expression.
