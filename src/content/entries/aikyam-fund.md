Aikyam works with early-stage organisations working on urgent challenges faced by the planet and underserved communities. Through its Aikyam Fellows programme, it makes fundraising, team and board building, and capacity development more accessible to changemakers. Samagata Foundation backs Aikyam to strengthen the front line of social change from the ground up.
