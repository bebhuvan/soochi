UPI now processes over 15 billion transactions a month — this is the single best high-frequency indicator of digital adoption and informal economic activity in India. NPCI publishes bank-wise and product-wise breakdowns monthly. The broader Retail Payment Statistics page covers IMPS, NACH, AePS, and NETC (FASTag) as well.
