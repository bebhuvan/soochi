Global Humanitarian Overview is the annual annual summary for global humanitarian need and response capacity built from multiple OCHA products. For researchers and nonprofits, it is most useful for funding context, identifying priority countries and framing macro-level needs versus available resources. It is not granular like district dashboards, but it gives the narrative and evidence baseline for grant proposals, policy letters and advocacy around humanitarian attention.
