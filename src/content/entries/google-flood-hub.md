Google Flood Hub maps current river conditions and forecasts from Google's
riverine flood models, including higher-confidence physical gauges and wider
coverage from virtual gauges. Forecasts extend up to seven days, while
inundation-probability layers are available in supported areas. It does not
cover flash or coastal floods, does not produce urban flood maps, and should
not be used as the sole source during an emergency; check government and local
weather-service warnings as well.
