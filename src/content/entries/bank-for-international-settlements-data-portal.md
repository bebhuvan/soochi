The BIS Data Portal is the central source for macro-financial indicators published by the Bank for International Settlements. It brings together time series and dashboards across banking, debt securities, derivatives, liquidity, asset prices, and related cross-border statistics.

For India-facing work, the portal is mostly useful for international comparability and global market context rather than India-only reporting. It is best used alongside national sources when a country-specific breakdown is needed.
