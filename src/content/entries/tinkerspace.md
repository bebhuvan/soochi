TinkerSpace is a not-for-profit community space for technology enthusiasts, tinkerers, coders, and hackers, situated near the CUSAT university campus in Kochi. Operating 24×7 and open to all, it hosts interdisciplinary workshops and events regularly. It is the physical evolution of the TinkerHub tech community that has operated across Kerala's colleges for close to a decade. Samagata Foundation backs the running of the space.
