YouthPower India publishes district-level rankings and trend views for youth employment, education, and skilling across India. It is useful for quickly identifying high- and low-performing districts, and for comparing states on youth opportunity indicators in a format suitable for policy and advocacy work.

The site is a dashboard-style service rather than a downloadable microdata source; most use is for monitoring trends and supporting further data deep dives from official sources.
