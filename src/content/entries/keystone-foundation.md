Keystone Foundation has built a cluster of sister enterprises: Aadhimalai (indigenous producer company), Last Forest (social enterprise for wild honey), Nilgiri Field Learning Centre, Radio Kotagiri, and the Honey Portal. Its model incubates producer companies and enterprises rather than limiting itself to short-cycle projects.
