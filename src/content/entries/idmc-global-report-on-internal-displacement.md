The Global Report on Internal Displacement (GRID) is the primary annual reference on internal displacement, which is often missed by India-focused migration and welfare analysis. For civic groups, it helps separate conflict-driven displacement from climate-disaster displacement and links both to protection and service-delivery outcomes. In policy cycles, GRID is used to test whether claims on social protection and housing burdens are consistent with independent displacement data. The publication is especially useful for triangulating with MHA, ReliefWeb, and household income or poverty datasets.
