Wetlands International South Asia coordinates the Asian Waterbird Census, publishes wetland management planning manuals, and developed the WIAMS framework (Inventory, Assessment and Monitoring framework for Indian Wetlands). Its wise-use framework centres the Ramsar concept as a guiding philosophy for wetland conservation and livelihood compatibility.
