The Organized Crime and Corruption Reporting Project (OCCRP) is an international non-profit investigative journalism organization founded in 2006. Operating across six continents, OCCRP connects independent media outlets to investigate cross-border financial crime, money laundering, high-level political kleptocracy, and environmental looting.

OCCRP develops and maintains Aleph, an open-source data indexing and cross-referencing platform housing over 400 million public and leaked records. Its investigations have led to asset seizures, resignations of corrupt officials, and international sanctions enforcement.
