Metabase is an open-source business intelligence and dashboarding tool designed to make data querying accessible to non-technical users. It connects to relational databases and data lakes, offering an intuitive visual query builder that lets users filter, group, aggregate, and chart data without writing SQL.

Widely deployed across non-profits, civic tech projects, and social enterprises, Metabase enables field officers, researchers, and project managers to track real-time program indicators, generate scheduled email/Slack reports, and publish public embeds of data trends.
