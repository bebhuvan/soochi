CourtsDaily structures public judicial records into searchable case timelines,
hearings, orders, parties, advocates and outcomes, with links back to official
court records. Coverage begins in 2017 for the National Company Law Tribunal
and in 2020 for most covered constitutional courts; consult the coverage page
before treating results as complete. Some services require an account or
subscription, and the terms prohibit systematic extraction of the database.
