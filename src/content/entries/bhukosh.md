Bhukosh is the Geological Survey of India's geospatial gateway for published
geoscience information. It brings map-indexed geology, mineral occurrences,
geochemistry, geophysics, exploration and thematic layers together for searching,
visual inspection and, where permitted, download or web-map use.

Coverage and scale vary substantially across layers, and a map suitable for
regional interpretation may be inappropriate for parcel- or site-level claims.
The portal can be slow or temporarily unreachable, and some functions require
registration. Check each layer's edition, scale, metadata and access conditions;
official publication does not automatically make every geoscience product an
openly reusable dataset.
