The Long House Collective (LHC) is a community art space operating from within a historic repurposed go-down in Calicut's small but burgeoning beachside art district. Founded by indie artist and curator Sherin, the space kick-started a thriving art community through conversations, gatherings, exhibitions, and workshops. It also serves as an artist residency. Samagata Foundation backed the setup of the space in 2023.
