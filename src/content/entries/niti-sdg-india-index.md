The SDG India Index is a practical benchmark for tracking state-level progress and regressions across most social and ecological SDG goals. It is especially useful for cross-state comparisons, where administrative data quality varies and advocacy teams need a common framework. The report side includes methodological notes and composite scores that can help structure proposals for municipal or district planning debates. For researchers, this is a strong entry point before moving into ministry-specific data extraction.
