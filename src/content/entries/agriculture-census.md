Conducted roughly every five years, the Agriculture Census describes operational
holdings rather than ownership parcels. It provides distributions by holding
size, social group and sex of holder, alongside operated area, tenancy, land use,
irrigation and cropping characteristics at national, state and finer levels where
tables are released.

An operational holding can combine multiple plots and may differ from the legal
owner or cadastral parcel, which matters when interpreting land concentration.
The newest portal also supports fieldwork and progress monitoring, while final
tables can arrive well after the reference year. Check whether a result is a
provisional dashboard count or a published census table.
