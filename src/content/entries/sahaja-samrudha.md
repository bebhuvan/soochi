Sahaja Samrudha is a Karnataka farmers' movement working to conserve
agrobiodiversity and make ecological agriculture viable. It connects seed
savers and organic farmers, supports community seed banks, conducts
participatory research and seed festivals, and works on traditional varieties
of rice, millets, vegetables, pulses, cotton and other crops.

Sahaja Seeds is the farmer-owned producer company and brand created to market
traditional, organically grown, open-pollinated seed. Env Wiki's old
`sahajaseeds.in` link has since been taken over by an unrelated hybrid-seed
business; this entry uses the verified Sahaja Samrudha and `sahajaseeds.com`
sites so the historical listing does not send users to the wrong organisation.
