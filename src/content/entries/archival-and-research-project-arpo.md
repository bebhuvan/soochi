ARPO (Archival and Research Project) is based in Kerala and works to preserve and promote local culture and heritage through digital archiving, multimedia storytelling, research, and community engagement. Its Earthlore project promotes fading tribal traditions and culture using music, videos, events, and informational content. Backed by Samagata Foundation.
