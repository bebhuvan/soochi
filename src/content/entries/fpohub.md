FPOhub is an initiative of the Centre for Sustainable Agriculture and Grameen
Academy intended as a shared resource for Farmer Producer Organisations. It
collects guidance, policy material, studies and discussion around forming and
strengthening farmer collectives, connecting them to markets and building
ecologically and economically sustainable rural livelihoods.

The site combines a resource library with a community forum rather than acting
as an official registry of every FPO. Several prominent resources are older and
download metadata can be incomplete, so users should verify policy documents
against current government sources and check dates before relying on operational
guidance.
