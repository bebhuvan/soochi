B.PAC (Bangalore Political Action Committee) runs civic-governance campaigns, leadership programmes (B.CLIP), safety audits (B.SAFE), and a Participatory Democracy Fellowship. Its Agenda for Bangalore framework operates as a durable civic reform platform.
