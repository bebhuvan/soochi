Gram Manchitra is the geographic complement to eGramSwaraj's financial data. It enables Gram Panchayats to map existing assets (schools, anganwadis, health centres, roads, water sources), plan new infrastructure, and visualise development works spatially. For researchers, it provides a window into how local infrastructure is distributed and planned at the most granular governance level. The spatial data layers include administrative boundaries, infrastructure points, and natural features at Panchayat scale. Combine with eGramSwaraj financial data for a complete picture of local governance.
