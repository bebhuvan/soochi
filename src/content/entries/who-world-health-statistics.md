World Health Statistics is the WHO’s consolidated annual release on global health indicators, including mortality, systems strength, risk factors and health outcomes. For India, it is useful both as a cross-country comparator and as a secondary validation layer for programme claims. The publication is especially useful for NGOs that need a stable source for indicators used in policy papers and public health monitoring templates.
