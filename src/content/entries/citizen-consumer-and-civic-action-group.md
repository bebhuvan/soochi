The Citizen consumer and civic Action Group (CAG) was established in 1985 in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, as an independent, non-profit citizen action group.

CAG conducts public interest research, policy advocacy, and community outreach on electricity sector reforms (representing consumer interests at state electricity regulatory commissions), public road safety enforcement, municipal solid waste management, and sustainable urban transport planning.
