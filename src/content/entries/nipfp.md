The National Institute of Public Finance and Policy (NIPFP) is an autonomous research institution under India's Ministry of Finance, established in 1976. It functions as the premier center for policy research and advisory on public economics, taxation, government expenditure, and fiscal federalism.

NIPFP's faculty and economists produce empirical working papers, assist Finance Commissions with revenue projections, analyze GST implementation dynamics, and evaluate fiscal deficit targets across Union and state governments.
