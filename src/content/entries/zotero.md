Zotero is the essential reference management tool for researchers — it captures citations from websites, journal databases, and library catalogues with a single click, organises them into collections, and generates formatted bibliographies in any citation style. For Indian researchers, Zotero's value goes beyond convenience: it enables building shared group libraries for research teams, annotating PDFs with notes, and automatically extracting metadata from Indian publications that use standard identifiers. The tool is free and open-source, developed by the Corporation for Digital Scholarship. It syncs across devices and integrates with Word, Google Docs, and LibreOffice. The Zotero Connector browser extension works with most Indian journal and government websites.
