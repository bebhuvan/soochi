Prayas (Energy Group) is an independent, non-profit research and advocacy group founded in Pune in 1990. It works to advance public interest in the energy sector through rigorous analysis of regulatory frameworks, power purchase agreements, renewable energy transitions, and electricity distribution company (DISCOM) finances.

Prayas actively engages in electricity regulatory commissions across Indian states, submitting data-driven interventions on consumer tariffs, rural electrification quality, appliance energy efficiency, and just energy transitions.
