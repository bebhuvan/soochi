Oorvani Foundation runs Citizen Matters (civic media platform), Open City (urban data commons), and the Civic Learning Hub (workshops, datajams, hackathons). Its three-step changemaking process — sensemaking, strengthening agency, problem solving — links information, data, resources, and networks so citizens can move from understanding to action.
