The Policy Edge (policyedge.in) is an independent, non-partisan public policy platform that bridges academic research, frontline implementation, and public understanding in India.

The platform publishes rapid policy explainers (Policy Bites), working papers authored by scholars from institutions including the IITs and IIMs, and direct field perspectives from civil servants and grassroots practitioners. It also conducts capacity-building programs, including executive courses in policy analysis, evaluation, and quantitative modelling in partnership with IIT Bombay.
