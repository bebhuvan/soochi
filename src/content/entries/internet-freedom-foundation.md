IFF (Internet Freedom Foundation) is India's leading digital rights organisation, defending the open internet through strategic litigation, parliamentary engagement, and public advocacy. It has been at the centre of almost every major Indian digital rights battle: the net neutrality campaign (Save the Internet), challenges to the IT Rules and internet shutdowns, surveillance reform (Pegasus), data protection law advocacy, and defending encryption. IFF runs the IFF Legal programme providing pro-bono legal support in digital rights cases, and publishes detailed explainers, policy analyses, and transparency reports. For researchers, journalists, and civic technologists, IFF's website is the most comprehensive archive of Indian digital rights law, policy, and litigation — and a model for rights-based tech advocacy globally.
