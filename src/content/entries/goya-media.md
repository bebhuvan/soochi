Goya is a dynamic media company focused on celebrating food, culture, and the stories that shape them. Its award-winning publication, The Goya Journal, champions regional cuisines and home cooks through photo essays, podcasts, videos, and long-form narratives. The 1000 Kitchens project documents heirloom recipes from across India as an open-source digital archive of intangible culinary heritage. Samagata Foundation supports 1000 Kitchens to scale its impact.
