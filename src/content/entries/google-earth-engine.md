Google Earth Engine (GEE) is a cloud-computing platform designed for planetary-scale environmental monitoring and geospatial data science.

GEE pairs an analysis-ready repository of over 90 petabytes of continuous historical satellite observations (Landsat, Sentinel, MODIS, ERA5 climate reanalysis, and global elevation models) with Google's distributed computing infrastructure. It provides Python and JavaScript APIs for pixel-level time-series analysis, land-use mapping, deforestation monitoring, and water surface tracking.
