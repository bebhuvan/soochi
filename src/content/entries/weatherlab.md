WeatherLab is an experimental atmospheric research platform by Google DeepMind and Google Research that visualizes predictions from state-of-the-art machine learning weather forecasting models.

The platform provides unified spatial layers for temperature, wind, and precipitation, and specializes in multi-ensemble tracking of tropical cyclones. It operationalizes foundational models including GraphCast, GenCast, and the flagship WeatherNext 3 ensemble system.
