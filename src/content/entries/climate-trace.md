Climate TRACE (Tracking Real-time Atmospheric Carbon Emissions) uses satellite imagery, remote sensing, and machine learning to estimate emissions from every significant source on Earth. Unlike national inventories that rely on self-reporting, TRACE independently observes emissions from 745M+ assets. For India, it provides facility-level estimates for power plants, steel mills, cement plants, ships, and landfills — making it a powerful accountability complement to official BUR submissions.
