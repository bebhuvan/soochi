Docmost is an open-source, self-hosted collaborative documentation and wiki platform created as an open alternative to proprietary tools like Notion and Confluence. Built with NestJS, Vue.js, and TipTap, it features real-time collaborative rich-text editing, nested page hierarchies, diagram support (Mermaid), and permissions management.

For research groups, non-profits, and civic tech teams, Docmost provides a private, internally-hosted workspace to document institutional standard operating procedures (SOPs), project meeting notes, and research findings with full data sovereignty.
