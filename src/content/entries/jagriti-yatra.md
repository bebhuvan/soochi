Jagriti Yatra is a nonprofit programme built around a long-distance train
journey through small-town and rural India. Participants meet social and
business entrepreneurs, develop enterprise ideas and join an alumni network
focused on employment and enterprise-led development beyond the largest
metropolitan centres.

The journey is selective rather than an open public service: prospective
participants apply, and programme dates, fees and routes can change each year.
Its environmental connection is only one part of a broader portfolio spanning
agriculture, health, technology, livelihoods and inclusive entrepreneurship,
which is why Soochi classifies it primarily under work, education and welfare.
