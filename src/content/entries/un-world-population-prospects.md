The World Population Prospects is the UN's biennial population reference. The 2024 revision provides estimates (1950-2023) and projections (2024-2100) for 237 countries, including India. Data includes total population, age and sex structure, fertility rates, life expectancy, international migration, and urban/rural population shares. For India, the 2023 estimate of 1.43 billion people surpassing China made global headlines — but WPP data supports far more granular questions about India's demographic dividend, ageing curve, urbanisation trajectory, and state-level population projections.
