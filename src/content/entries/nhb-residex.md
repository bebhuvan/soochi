The only systematic housing price dataset for Indian cities. NHB RESIDEX tracks price indices for 50 cities (26 from 2010, 24 added later), separately reporting under-construction and completed property prices. Also publishes the HPI@AssessmentPrice — a valuation-based index using actual bank lending data. Essential for urban economics, housing policy, and real estate research.
