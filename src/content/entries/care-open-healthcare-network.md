CARE is modular health infrastructure for facilities, hospitals and public
health networks. It supports electronic patient records, encounters, facility
and resource management, clinical workflows, critical-care coordination and
analytics, with extensions for tele-ICU, home care and other service models.

The platform is maintained by the Open Healthcare Network Foundation and is
listed as a Digital Public Good. Its code is free and MIT-licensed, but a safe
production deployment requires clinical governance, secure hosting,
configuration, training and compliance with applicable health-data rules.
