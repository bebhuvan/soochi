Courtyard Koota in Kengeri, Bengaluru, is a community and event space that knits together art, music, science, and culture. Samagata Foundation supports Makkala Masti, its outreach programme combining education, arts, and theatre for government school children in surrounding villages. The programme employs dedicated teachers and provides children access to the space's varied activities.
