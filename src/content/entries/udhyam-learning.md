Udhyam runs two large programmes: Udhyam Shiksha (entrepreneurial mindset curriculum for government school students) and Udhyam Vyapaar (nano-entrepreneurship support). Its Bet On Yourself philosophy centres on building grit, self-awareness, and practical problem-solving. The Istri Project helped ironing vendors switch from coal to LPG.
