The Watershed Organisation Trust (WOTR) is a non-profit organization established in 1993 in Pune, Maharashtra. WOTR develops ecosystem-based adaptation strategies for drought-prone and rainfed agricultural communities across multiple Indian states.

Through its applied research arm, the WOTR Centre for Resilience Studies (W-CReS), the organisation designs participatory water budgeting methods, farm-level agro-meteorological advisory systems, and watershed restoration interventions that rebuild degraded soils and protect rural household livelihoods against climate shocks.
