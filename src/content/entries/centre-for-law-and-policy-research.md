The Centre for Law and Policy Research (CLPR) is a non-profit trust based in Bengaluru, founded in 2009 by Jayna Kothari and Sudhir Krishnaswamy. CLPR combines public interest litigation with historical and empirical legal scholarship to advance constitutional protections and social rights.

CLPR built ConstitutionofIndia.net (CADIndia), the canonical searchable digital archive of India's Constituent Assembly Debates. The organisation actively litigates landmark constitutional cases on transgender rights, the rights of persons with disabilities, and intersectional discrimination under Indian law.
