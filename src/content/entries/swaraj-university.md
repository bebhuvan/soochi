Swaraj University is an alternative learning community centred on self-designed
education. Participants, called khojis, build individual learning plans around
real projects, peer cohorts, reflection and apprenticeships with practitioners,
rather than following a standard degree curriculum or examination sequence.

The programme connects personal learning with ecological responsibility,
community life, livelihoods and critical examination of conventional education.
It is not a degree-granting university in the conventional regulatory sense;
participation involves an application process and programme commitments, which
should be understood before treating it as a substitute for an accredited course.
