Opened in 1989, JNP is a premier science centre in Bengaluru. Samagata Foundation supports three key initiatives: Taralaya (fully funded day trips for rural government school children), REAP (a rigorous three-year weekend science programme where 135+ alumni have earned PhDs), and the Telescope Borrowing Program for amateur astronomers. Samagata set up an endowment providing REAP scholarships for 20 years.
