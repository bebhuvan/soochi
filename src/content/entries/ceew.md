The Council on Energy, Environment and Water (CEEW) is one of South Asia's leading non-profit policy research institutions. It uses data and integrated analysis to advise on renewable energy scale-up, industrial decarbonisation, air quality management, power sector transitions, and climate risk vulnerability.

CEEW builds interactive public tools and dashboards — including the India Renewables Dashboard, Electric Mobility Dashboard, ValueSTAC solar calculator, and climate vulnerability atlases — converting sectoral research into open decision-support infrastructure.
