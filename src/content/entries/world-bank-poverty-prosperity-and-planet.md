The Poverty and Shared Prosperity reports focus on growth quality, inequality and opportunity gaps, with practical pathways for translating macro growth into welfare gains. For India, this is useful for comparing state and household-level welfare trajectories against broader development strategy and climate adaptation pressures. It is frequently used in development briefs where poverty is being discussed alongside financing constraints and social protection design.
