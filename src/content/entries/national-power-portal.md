The National Power Portal consolidates operational and historical information
from electricity generation and transmission utilities. Its report library
covers station- and fuel-wise generation, installed capacity, outages, coal and
gas supply, hydro reservoirs, transmission lines, substations, electricity
consumption and demand at all-India, regional and state levels.

Many reports are downloadable in both XLS and PDF, making the report library
more reusable than the visual dashboard. Some dashboard panels retain stale
labels or placeholders even while the published-report archive is current, so
prefer dated files and record whether a figure is tentative, actual, daily or
monthly.
