The official Union Budget site publishes the Budget at a Glance, receipts and
expenditure statements, demands for grants, the Finance Bill, fiscal-policy
statements and ministry or scheme allocations. Its archive is the primary place
to retrieve the documents as presented to Parliament, with spreadsheet versions
available for many current tables.

Budget estimates, revised estimates and actual expenditure are different
measures and should not be mixed. The site also warns that its system-generated
Excel files are provided for convenience and the signed or published PDF is
authoritative if values conflict. For cleaned historical series, compare this
source with Open Budgets India and RBI DBIE.
