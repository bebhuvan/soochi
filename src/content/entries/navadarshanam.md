Navadarshanam is a public charitable trust and rural community near the
Tamil Nadu–Karnataka border. It treats its campus as a continuing experiment in
lower-impact living, working across organic food, renewable energy, water,
building methods, waste and the relationship between ecological practice and
community life.

The trust also runs educational programmes and initiatives intended to support
nearby small and marginal farmers. It is both a place and an organisation: the
residential community carries out daily work, while a board of trustees holds
the land and formal responsibility. Visitors should check current programme and
stay information rather than assuming unrestricted public access to the campus.
