Established in 2008, MHAT provides mental health support to communities through a decentralised, low-cost model relying on volunteer support and task-sharing among professionals and trained non-professionals. It performs economic screening and provides services free of charge, with patient tracking, outreach, and regular training. Samagata Foundation supports MHAT's Community Mental Health Clinics in Bengaluru.
