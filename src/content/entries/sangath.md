Sangath is an Indian non-profit healthcare and research organization founded in 1996 in Goa. It is internationally recognized for its pioneering scholarship in global mental health, child development, and adolescent well-being.

Sangath specializes in "task-sharing" models — developing and testing evidence-based psychological interventions that can be delivered by trained non-specialist community health workers rather than scarce psychiatrists. Its clinical trials on maternal depression, alcohol use disorders, and autism have shaped WHO mental health guidelines worldwide.
