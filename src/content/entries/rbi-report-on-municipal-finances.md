The Report on Municipal Finances complements state-level fiscal work by drilling into how municipalities finance operations, manage debt and handle transfer dependence. For civic organisations supporting cities, this report is especially useful for understanding structural gaps in property tax buoyancy, service delivery costs and urban borrowing constraints. The annual series gives a national baseline and a useful counterpoint to city-specific budgets or state finance statements, especially during urban reform or fiscal devolution discussions.
