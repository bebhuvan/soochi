The Impact Ecosystem Map is a free network map from 60 Decibels, an impact measurement company spun out of Acumen in 2019. It links impact funds and funders to the companies in their portfolios, covering more than 10,000 entities.

Use it to see who has invested in whom, to find co-investors in a company or sector, and to spot clusters of capital in areas such as off-grid energy, financial inclusion, agriculture and health. Entities can be filtered and sorted by name or by number of connections.

The map is a companion to 60dB Signal, the company's paid platform of customer-survey data on impact companies, and that deeper company data sits behind a sales conversation. The investment links come from self-reported and public information, and the map itself warns that they may be incomplete or wrong, so confirm any link before relying on it. Organisations can submit additions and corrections.
