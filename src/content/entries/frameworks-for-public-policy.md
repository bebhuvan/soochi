Frameworks for Public Policy is a growing reference library of mental models
drawn from books, papers and more than 300 editions of the *Anticipating the
Unintended* newsletter. Its short, plain-language entries help readers define
policy problems, distinguish outcomes from spending, choose policy instruments
and account for political and institutional constraints.

The collection spans public policy, political thinking, public finance, foreign
policy and geopolitics, society, and general-purpose models. Readers can browse
by subject or describe a problem to find relevant frameworks. It is curated by
Pranay Kotasthane and published as a Takshashila Institution initiative.
