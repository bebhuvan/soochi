Veditum's flagship Moving Upstream series walks along rivers to build public records, documentaries, data archives, and field-based evidence. Other artefacts include India Sand Watch, the Moving Upstream Fellowship, and the Life along River Ken colouring series. It works at the intersection of environment, culture, society, and media.
