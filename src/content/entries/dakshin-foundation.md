Dakshin Foundation runs the Andaman Nicobar Environment Team (ANET), ReefLog and LTEO monitoring, Lagoon Fest, and the SeaChange intersectoral model for coastal socio-ecological change. Its #KhaneKeLiyeBachao philosophy ties food, livelihoods, and ecological sustainability into a rights-based conservation approach.
