Coal generates ~70% of India's electricity and is the single largest source of government mineral revenue. The CCO publishes monthly output and dispatch data for Coal India, SCCL, and captive mines, with grade-wise and state-wise breakdowns. The Ministry of Coal's broader statistics suite adds import, export, and washery data.
