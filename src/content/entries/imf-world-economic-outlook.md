The World Economic Outlook is where the IMF publishes core growth, inflation, current account and fiscal outlooks used in policy and media discussions. For India, it is often the quickest cross-country source for comparable macro assumptions in election-year and policy analysis cycles. Researchers value the regular methodology notes and indicator history; civil society groups use it to ground arguments around debt, growth quality, and global spillovers into domestic inflation or employment outcomes.
