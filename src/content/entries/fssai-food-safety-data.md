FSSAI publishes annual reports with state-wise data on food samples collected, tested, found non-conforming, and adulterated; civil and criminal enforcement actions; licensing and registration statistics; and foodborne illness outbreak data. The FoSCoS portal adds real-time food business operator licensing data.
