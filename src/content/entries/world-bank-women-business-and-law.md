Women, Business and the Law compares legal and regulatory differences that affect women’s participation in the economy across 190 economies. For India-focused research, it complements labour and welfare sources by showing how legal frameworks shape access to work, asset ownership and entrepreneurship. The publication is strong for advocacy documents because it gives a global baseline for legal gaps and a clear set of indicators that policymakers can act on.
