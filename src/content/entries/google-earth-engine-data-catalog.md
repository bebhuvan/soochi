Earth Engine hosts over 1,000 public datasets: full Landsat and Sentinel archives, MODIS, ERA5, CHIRPS rainfall, WorldPop, Global Forest Change, Dynamic World land cover, and dozens more. What makes it unique is that the data lives alongside a planetary-scale compute service — you don't download petabytes; you query it in place. Free for research, education, and non-commercial use. The de facto platform for geospatial analysis at scale.
