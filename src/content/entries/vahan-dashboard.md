VAHAN aggregates vehicle-registration transactions from transport offices and
lets users break registrations down by vehicle class, fuel, maker, model, state,
office and time period. It is particularly useful for studying electric-vehicle
adoption, motorisation, commercial fleets and changes in the composition of new
registrations.

Registrations are not the same as vehicles currently on the road, sales or
vehicle kilometres travelled. Administrative onboarding and backfilled records
can also affect trends. The long-running VAHAN4 dashboard is scheduled to be
discontinued after 15 August 2026; this entry points to its new public analytics
home and keeps the legacy link for continuity.
