The DHAN Foundation (Development of Humane Action) was founded in 1997 in Madurai, Tamil Nadu, to eliminate poverty through institution-building and community ownership.

DHAN is recognized for its *Vayalagam Tankfed Agriculture Development Programme*, which rehabilitates ancient village tank cascade systems and establishes democratic Water Users Associations to manage local surface water. It also created the *Kalanjiam Community Banking Programme*, federating women's self-help groups into self-sustaining micro-finance institutions.
