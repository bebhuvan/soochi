Based at the Paris School of Economics, the World Inequality Lab studies
inequality within and between countries and develops public tools for using
that research. It hosts and maintains the World Inequality Database, publishes
reports and methodological handbooks, and runs projects on income, wealth,
gender, political, tax and environmental inequality. WID includes downloadable
country series for India as well as cross-country comparisons.
