The Centre for Economic Data and Analysis (CEDA) is an economics research hub and open data platform at Ashoka University dedicated to making India's socio-economic data accessible, intuitive, and visually actionable.

CEDA integrates and harmonizes complex government data collections (including NSS, PLFS, NFHS, Census, ASI, ASUSE, and AGMARKNET). It offers interactive visual query tools, including:
- **Socio-Economic Data Portal:** Multi-dimensional cross-tabulation across health, education, employment, gender, and demographics.
- **Agri-Market & Food Prices Trackers:** Longitudinal commodity price and arrival trends across 2,700+ agricultural mandis.
- **Economic Enterprises Data Tracker:** Longitudinal profiles of formal and informal manufacturing and services enterprises.
- **Data Notes & Policy Briefs:** Methodological explanations and empirical analysis clarifying nuances in official statistics.
